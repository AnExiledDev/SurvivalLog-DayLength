# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- The day length can be changed on the Mod Manager's Mods screen without restarting the game. A new length takes effect the next time you load a save.
- The Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer.

## 0.1.0

First public release. Makes each in-game day last longer in real time, twice as long by default. Hunger, plant growth and disaster countdowns slow down with it.
